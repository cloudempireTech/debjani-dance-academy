## 3. Core Portfolio Offerings
1. Hip-Hop & Urban Street Dance: Breaking, popping, locking, lyrical hip-hop, dynamic footwork, and groove control.
2. Indian Classical & Creative: Kathak rhythm, footwork (tatkar), mudras, abhinaya, and Rabindra Nritya expressions.
3. Western Contemporary & Modern: Fluid floor work, contemporary lyrical storytelling, flexibility, and expressive movement.
4. Wedding Sangeet & Celebration Choreography: Bespoke couple dances, family group sangeet routines, flash mobs, and festive stage concepts.
5. Kids Dance Foundation: Fun, rhythm awareness, motor coordination, and confidence building for children aged 4+.
6. Dance Fitness & Movement Meditation: High-energy cardio dance sessions combined with mindful movement and posture alignment.
