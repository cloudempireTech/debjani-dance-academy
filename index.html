Studio Ethos

A Family of Expressive Dancers in Thakurpukur

Founded under the artistic direction of Guru Debjani, our academy stands as a recognized creative sanctuary in Purba Barisha. We celebrate individuality, self-confidence, and artistic discipline in an encouraging atmosphere where every student is nurtured like family.

✓ Mirrored Dance Studio: Acoustic sound system, smooth wooden flooring, and spacious rehearsal area.
✓ Flexible Class Formats: Weekend batches, weekday evenings, online hybrid sessions, and private home choreography.
✓ Stage Recitals & Certifications: Annual stage shows, board examination guidance, and inter-academy competitions.
